# Monita 1.2.0

Monita 1.2.0 makes notification behavior more deliberate: workflow controls are opt-in per message, Notification Channels expire messages after 24 hours by default, and administrator cleanup treats an image-bearing notification as one complete message.

## Per-message controls

Messages may individually enable:

- **Assign to Me**
- **Resolve / Reopen**
- **Attach files or images**

The controls are carried in `monita::messageControls`, returned in message collaboration metadata, and enforced by the server. Messages that do not request a control do not expose it.

## Retention

- Every existing Notification Channel is migrated once to 24-hour retention on upgrade.
- New Notification Channels default to 24 hours.
- Chat Channels remain indefinite by default.
- Archived Notification messages expire on the same schedule; archiving does not extend lifetime.
- Retention uses exact 24-hour periods from the message timestamp.

## Images and deletion

- Administrator **Delete** permanently removes the entire message and all included image/attachment metadata.
- Associated stored attachment files are cleaned immediately after a permanent delete.
- Retention removes message attachment records and the maintenance cycle removes their files.
- Administrator **Archive** keeps the complete message and image together until restored, deleted, or expired.
- Chat attachment types remain JPEG, PNG, GIF, and WebP.
